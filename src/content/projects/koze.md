## What it is

KOZE is an Indonesian basics apparel label. Its site works as a catalog rather than a shop: visitors browse the collection, check product availability, and contact the admin to buy. The announcement bar says it plainly: this is a catalog, and orders happen in conversation.

That decision shapes everything else. There is no cart or payment flow to design around. The page has to make the clothes easy to scan, explain the brand, and make the next step obvious.

## What I worked on

- The storefront structure: navigation, collection pages and the path from the homepage to a product.
- The product presentation: a dense image grid, collection banners and mobile layouts that keep the clothing legible.
- The supporting pages: store location, contact, and the journal.
- The hand-off to the admin through WhatsApp and the catalog notice.

The result is a Shopify storefront, so most of the work sits in theme structure, Liquid templates, CSS and small JavaScript interactions rather than a custom backend.

## The trade-off

A catalog without checkout removes a lot of scope: no payment integration, no shipping rules, no order states. It also adds a manual step for the customer. Whether that is acceptable depends on the business. For a label that already sells through distributors and wants a browsable public catalog, the simpler path is defensible.

I would not use this model for a brand that expects direct online sales. The trade-off is not "less features"; it is a different sales process.

## What I learned

- Product hierarchy matters more than decoration. A catalog lives or dies on how quickly someone can find the right item.
- A clear next step can be a conversation. Not every commerce site needs a checkout to be useful.
- Theme work is still systems work: templates, reusable sections and responsive rules have to agree with each other.

## Status

The public storefront is live at [kozeid.com](https://kozeid.com). The earlier staging host used during the project is no longer resolving, so the production site is the reliable reference.
